<p>Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS) and Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis (TEN) are life-threatening conditions that sometimes are caused by a drug reaction.  SJS and TEN can involve a <strong>severe</strong> <strong>adverse reaction to medication</strong>. As Julie says, anyone can get it in reaction to any drug, even Over The Counter (OTC) medications like children&#8217;s motrin/ibuprofen.</p>
<p>Holding a drug manufacturer legally responsible for putting a dangerous drug on the market (or failure to adequately warn) is challenging. Consider the case of Sabrina Brierton Johnson, whose family <a href="http://www.boston.com/business/healthcare/articles/2008/07/18/jury_finds_for_jj_in_motrin_suit/" target="_blank">unsuccessfully sued</a> the manufacturer of Children&#8217;s Motrin, after SJS blinded her. Contacting the right attorney (and striking the right jury) is everything.</p>
<p>If you think your child may have a claim against a manufacturer of a drug responsible for causing your child to contract SJS or TEN, let&#8217;s talk about what would be involved in a Stevens-Johnson or Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis lawsuit.</p>
