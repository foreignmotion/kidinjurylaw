<p>It is with great expectations that I introduce my new website &#8211; <a title="PEARSON LAW FIRM WEBSITE" href="http://petepearsonlaw.com" target="_blank">www.petepearsonlaw.com</a>.</p>
<p>It was designed by the guys over at <a title="KMDIG WEBSITE" href="http://kmdig.com" target="_blank">kmdig.com</a>, a creative advertising, interactive, and branding agency.</p>
<p>Aside from the fresh look, the site offers you, as a prospective client, more information about me, my legal services, and even has a link to the <a title="PEARSON LAW FIRM ATTORNEY FEE AGREEMENT" href="http://petepearsonlaw.com/media/attorney-fee-agreement-new.pdf" target="_blank">attorney fee contract</a> you would sign if you retain me.</p>
<p>On top of that, I have a &#8220;NEWS&#8221; section that to be completely honest I added because my web designer said it would help with SEO. But since they persuaded me to add it, I will do my best to use it as a tool to benefit potential clients who go online to research legal questions.</p>
<p>I hope you will check it out. And offer me any feedback, positive or negative.</p>
<p>Thank you for reading.</p>
