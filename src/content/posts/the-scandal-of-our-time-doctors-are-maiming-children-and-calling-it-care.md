<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There is no polite way to say this anymore.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>What is being done to children in the name of “gender-affirming care” is one of the greatest medical scandals of our generation.</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Vulnerable kids—confused, depressed, anxious, and searching for identity—are being fast-tracked into&nbsp;<strong>irreversible medical interventions</strong>&nbsp;that will impact the rest of their lives.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And the people doing it?</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Doctors. Hospitals. Licensed professionals who swore an oath to do no harm.</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>THIS IS NOT COMPASSION &#8211; THIS IS MALPRACTICE.</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Children do not have fully developed judgment.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">They are not equipped to make life-altering decisions about:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Sterilization</li>



<li>Amputation of healthy body parts</li>



<li>Lifelong dependence on hormones</li>



<li>Permanent loss of biological function</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Yet across the country, medical providers are:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Prescribing puberty blockers</li>



<li>Administering cross-sex hormones</li>



<li>Performing irreversible surgeries</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">All under the banner of “affirmation.”</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>That is not careful medicine. That is ideological medicine.</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And when ideology replaces sound medical judgment,&nbsp;<strong>children get hurt.</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>FAMILIES ARE BEING CUT OUT.  ON PURPOSE.</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Even more disturbing, many parents are being:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Kept in the dark</li>



<li>Lied to</li>



<li>Or pushed aside entirely</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Schools, counselors, and providers are making life-altering decisions&nbsp;<strong>without full parental involvement</strong>—sometimes actively concealing what is happening.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">That is not just unethical.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>In many cases, it is legally actionable.</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We are now seeing the consequences:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">

<li>Patients facing lifelong medical complications</li>



<li>Families shattered by decisions that should never have been made</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And in the most tragic cases,&nbsp;<strong>children lost entirely</strong>&nbsp;after being failed by the very systems that were supposed to protect them.</p>
