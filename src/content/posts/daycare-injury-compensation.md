<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Daycares can be dangerous.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Take a recent case of mine for example. I was hired by a young mother whose 2-year-old daughter&nbsp;had suffered a serious injury while in daycare. The&nbsp;little girl&nbsp;was playing on the floor in a class of 23 other children overseen by two teachers. While the teachers were preoccupied with directing the children from breakfast to playtime, a much larger child&nbsp;got too rambunctious and&nbsp;fell on the little girl’s left leg.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The outcome? My client&#8217;s little girl went&nbsp;to the hospital with a fractured tibia.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Like I said, daycares can be dangerous.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If your child has been injured while in daycare, you may be wondering: &#8220;What legal options do I have? How does someone in my situation&nbsp;seek compensation?&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The answer to this question depends heavily on your particular circumstances. In the situation I described a moment ago, my client had a very strong case. However, this was&nbsp;<em>not&nbsp;</em>due primarily to the seriousness of the injury, but rather to the ability to clearly prove that the injury was a result of negligence on the part of the daycare. If your circumstances are similar, you may have a strong case as well.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong><em>Why Did My Client Have a Strong Case?</em></strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When my client first called me, I immediately advised her to file a complaint with the Georgia Department of Early Care and Learning, also known as Bright from the Start. This complaint would spark an investigation of the daycare, the findings of which would be incredibly important to my client&#8217;s case. As a daycare licensed by the state of Georgia, the center where my client’s daughter sustained her injury is required to meet state standards. If the daycare was not following state standards in the classroom where my client&#8217;s child was, then a clear line could be drawn from their rule-breaking to the child&#8217;s injury. The daycare would be clearly at fault for the incident.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As it turns out, this was the case.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Bright from the Start requires licensed daycares to follow specific teacher:child ratios that vary based on the age of the children, as follows:</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>Under 1 year or under 18 months if not walking = 1:6</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>1 year and walking = 1:8</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>2 years = 1:10</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>3 years = 1:15</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>4 years 1:18</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>5 years = 1:20</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>6 years and older = 1:25</em></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Bright from the Start’s investigation discovered that, on the day of the injury, the little girl’s classroom had a ratio of two teachers to twenty-four 2-year-old children. According to state regulations,&nbsp;<strong><em>an additional teacher should have been present.</em></strong>&nbsp;However, the daycare neglected to follow this important rule. Because of this, the two teachers that were present had their hands too full to keep close tabs on all the children. And as a result, my client’s daughter ended up with a fractured leg.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A clear line could be drawn from the daycare’s failure to abide by state standards to the injury that occurred. This fact made for a strong case as it clearly showed that the daycare was at fault. But there was an additional layer to the story.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Bright from the Start records revealed that this same daycare had also been caught violating state standards on numerous past occasions<em>. </em>Their records showed 11 previous violations of the staff:child ratio standards <strong><em>alone.</em></strong> This was a major boost for my client&#8217;s case as it proved a pattern of negligence on the part of the daycare. In court, a jury would likely see this pattern as grounds for punishing the daycare by awarding my client punitive damages on top of the general damages she would have already received. For this reason, the daycare’s insurer made an offer we couldn’t refuse, and my client received considerable compensation.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong><em>How does all this relate to YOU?</em></strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If your child was injured while in daycare, it is possible that the fault can be traced to the childcare center’s negligence in following state standards. If so, you may have a strong case. However, to prove negligence, the incident must be investigated, which is why it is very important that you file a complaint with Bright from the Start (Georgia Department of Early Care and Learning). You can&nbsp;get more information about how to file a complaint&nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="http://www.decal.ga.gov/Bfts/faq.aspx#:~:text=Bright%20from%20the%20Start%3A%20The,5957" target="_blank">HERE</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After filing a complaint, get in touch with an attorney. An attorney can help you navigate the complexities of your situation. Maybe the daycare is clearly at fault and in violation of state standards (of which there are many, as you can read about&nbsp;<a href="/2011/11/18/child-injury-at-day-care-using-georgia-licensing-standards-for-day-care-centers-to-prove-negligence/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">HERE</a>). Or maybe your daycare is exempt from licensure and is not required to follow state standards, which you can read about&nbsp;<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/2013/03/15/child-injury-at-unlicensed-or-exempt-daycare-centers-in-georgia/" target="_blank">HERE</a>. Whatever the details of your case, an attorney can help you navigate the many aspects of daycare injuries, providing clear expertise on the legal options available to you and your child.</p>
