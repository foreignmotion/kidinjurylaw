<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Teenagers and Driving &#8211; Automobile accident claims</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Sometimes teenagers are UNFAIRLY blamed for an auto accident. There is a tendency to blame teenagers for doing something wrong whenever they are involved in a crash. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Sometimes the police don&#8217;t listen to what the teenager says. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Sometimes the teenager doesn&#8217;t know how to communicate the truth of what happened.  Being hurt, they just don&#8217;t get the chance to share their side of the story. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Being charged for causing a car accident can have big consequences for a teenager. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">It is important to have an attorney on your side early in the process</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I represent teens who have been injured in a car accident due to the fault of another. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But sometimes fault is not crystal clear at the start of a legal claim. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Gathering evidence, interviewing eyewitnesses, talking to the investigating officers &#8211; all of that needs to happen in the days and weeks immediately after the crash. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The insurance company for the at-fault driver will have their investigators and lawyers working already &#8211; your child needs to have a lawyer to level the playing field. </p>
